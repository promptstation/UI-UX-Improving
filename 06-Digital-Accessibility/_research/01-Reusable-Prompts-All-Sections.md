STEP 1 — Reusable prompts for all sections
Course 06 — Digital Accessibility (a11y)
Task source: 00-Research-Outline-Course-06.md

You are free to install software, programs and write codes, research the internet so as to meet our goals.

---

**Section 1: WCAG Foundations — Principles and Conformance**

Task: POUR principles, A versus AA versus AAA targets, scoping audits by template, and writing conformance claims that survive scrutiny.

Absorbed skills:
- Scope audits by template
- target correct conformance levels
- write defensible conformance claims


---

**Section 2: Contrast and Color — 4.5:1 Discipline**

Task: Text and non-text contrast ratios, focus-indicator contrast, color-independence of meaning, and dark-mode contrast verification.

Absorbed skills:
- Verify 4.5:1 and 3:1 ratios everywhere
- remove color-only meaning
- prove dark-mode compliance


---

**Section 3: Keyboard Navigation — Focus Order and States**

Task: Logical tab order, visible focus states, skip links, focus traps for dialogs, and roving-tabindex widget patterns.

Absorbed skills:
- Build fully keyboard-operable flows
- show visible focus always
- implement correct dialog and widget patterns


---

**Section 4: Semantics and ARIA — Correct Roles**

Task: Native elements first, landmarks, widget roles, live regions for dynamic content, and the first rule of ARIA in practice.

Absorbed skills:
- Choose native semantics by default
- apply ARIA only where native falls short
- announce dynamic changes properly


---

**Section 5: Reflow and Resilience — 320px to 400 Percent Zoom**

Task: Reflow containers surviving 320px widths and 400 percent zoom: no two-dimensional scrolling, spacing overrides, and truncation discipline.

Absorbed skills:
- Build reflow-proof layouts
- pass spacing-override tests
- keep content reachable at extreme zoom


---

**Section 6: Motion, Timing and Media — Vestibular and Cognitive Safety**

Task: Reduced-motion handling, timeout and autoplay rules, captions and transcripts, and flashing safeguards for vestibular and cognitive safety.

Absorbed skills:
- Honor reduced-motion preferences
- caption all meaningful media
- eliminate timing and flashing hazards


---

**Section 7: Testing Systems — Automated plus Manual Audits**

Task: Layering axe-style automation with screen-reader passes and disabled-user testing, plus regression suites preventing reintroduction.

Absorbed skills:
- Combine automated and manual testing
- run screen-reader passes
- lock gains with regression tests


---

**Section 8: Capstone — Certified Accessible Experience**

Task: Full capstone: audited product remediated to AA with evidence, test reports, conformance statement, and regression protection.

Absorbed skills:
- Deliver AA-conformant experiences with evidence
- publish conformance statements
- prevent regression
