**Section 1: WCAG Foundations — Principles and Conformance — engineered prompt**

RESULT:

Develop a comprehensive, professional-level learning module on "WCAG Foundations — Principles and Conformance" as part of an advanced course in Digital Accessibility (a11y) for photograph editors and enhancers. The module must teach the following substance: POUR principles, A versus AA versus AAA targets, scoping audits by template, and writing conformance claims that survive scrutiny.

Build the capability to scope audits by template. Explain the governing principle, show the professional method step by step, demonstrate with realistic before-and-after cases, and name the exact failure that proves the capability is missing.

Build the capability to target correct conformance levels. Explain the governing principle, show the professional method step by step, demonstrate with realistic before-and-after cases, and name the exact failure that proves the capability is missing.

Build the capability to write defensible conformance claims. Explain the governing principle, show the professional method step by step, demonstrate with realistic before-and-after cases, and name the exact failure that proves the capability is missing.

Balance throughout the module the demands of correct method, protected critical qualities, honest limits, efficiency, and series coherence. Show how this section's concern interacts with neighboring sections without duplicating them.

Include practical working methods covering:

* Scope audits by template
* target correct conformance levels
* write defensible conformance claims
* Working method with axe-style automation, screen readers, contrast analyzers, and reflow test rigs
* Proof and QA passes at full size
* Series-consistency discipline
* Delivery documentation and rationale writing

Explain how practitioners can avoid common failure modes such as color-only meaning; invisible or missing focus states; ARIA roles native elements already provide; keyboard traps in dialogs; untested claims of accessibility. For each failure, trace the causal move and show the professional correction.

Use numerous realistic examples drawn from contexts such as checkout flow made fully keyboard-operable; dashboard passing 400 percent zoom; conformance statement surviving scrutiny. For each important principle, show weak, improved, and professional-level outcomes, and keep examples focused on this section's craft rather than generic editing advice.

Include practical exercises that require the learner to apply every capability target on real assignments. For each exercise provide the scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

Structure the material progressively from foundations to independent professional judgment. Use professional terminology from the relevant disciplines, explained in clear language, and never lapse into generic tips.

Research the subject using high-quality professional and academic sources where external research materially strengthens the material, prioritizing WCAG specifications, WAI-ARIA practices, and accessibility testing methodology. Avoid low-quality SEO listicles, preset-market blogs, and unsupported best-practice claims. Distinguish established findings from professional conventions and informed recommendations.

Create a professional framework usable on paid jobs, functioning simultaneously as learning resource, practical reference, and working methodology. Where recommendations depend on context, explain the trade-off explicitly rather than stating absolute rules.

End the module with a "WCAG Foundations — Principles and Conformance checklist", a "Digital Accessibility (a11y) pre-delivery checklist", and a "series-consistency checklist" usable by a professional studio before work is approved.

The final result must be comprehensive enough to serve as advanced professional training, yet organized clearly enough for progressive study and immediate practical application.
