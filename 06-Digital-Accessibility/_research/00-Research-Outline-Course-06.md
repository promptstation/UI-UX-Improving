Course 6 Research — Digital Accessibility (a11y)

---

**Section 1: WCAG Foundations — Principles and Conformance**

**Summary:** POUR principles, A versus AA versus AAA targets, scoping audits by template, and writing conformance claims that survive scrutiny.

**Absorbed Skill:** Scope audits by template; target correct conformance levels; write defensible conformance claims.

**Section 2: Contrast and Color — 4.5:1 Discipline**

**Summary:** Text and non-text contrast ratios, focus-indicator contrast, color-independence of meaning, and dark-mode contrast verification.

**Absorbed Skill:** Verify 4.5:1 and 3:1 ratios everywhere; remove color-only meaning; prove dark-mode compliance.

**Section 3: Keyboard Navigation — Focus Order and States**

**Summary:** Logical tab order, visible focus states, skip links, focus traps for dialogs, and roving-tabindex widget patterns.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build fully keyboard-operable flows; show visible focus always; implement correct dialog and widget patterns.

**Section 4: Semantics and ARIA — Correct Roles**

**Summary:** Native elements first, landmarks, widget roles, live regions for dynamic content, and the first rule of ARIA in practice.

**Absorbed Skill:** Choose native semantics by default; apply ARIA only where native falls short; announce dynamic changes properly.

**Section 5: Reflow and Resilience — 320px to 400 Percent Zoom**

**Summary:** Reflow containers surviving 320px widths and 400 percent zoom: no two-dimensional scrolling, spacing overrides, and truncation discipline.

**Absorbed Skill:** Build reflow-proof layouts; pass spacing-override tests; keep content reachable at extreme zoom.

**Section 6: Motion, Timing and Media — Vestibular and Cognitive Safety**

**Summary:** Reduced-motion handling, timeout and autoplay rules, captions and transcripts, and flashing safeguards for vestibular and cognitive safety.

**Absorbed Skill:** Honor reduced-motion preferences; caption all meaningful media; eliminate timing and flashing hazards.

**Section 7: Testing Systems — Automated plus Manual Audits**

**Summary:** Layering axe-style automation with screen-reader passes and disabled-user testing, plus regression suites preventing reintroduction.

**Absorbed Skill:** Combine automated and manual testing; run screen-reader passes; lock gains with regression tests.

**Section 8: Capstone — Certified Accessible Experience**

**Summary:** Full capstone: audited product remediated to AA with evidence, test reports, conformance statement, and regression protection.

**Absorbed Skill:** Deliver AA-conformant experiences with evidence; publish conformance statements; prevent regression.
